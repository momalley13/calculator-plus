Sensor & Signal Library

Temperature

RTD (Pt100/Pt1000, IEC 60751), Thermocouple, Thermistor. Typical outputs: 4–20 mA, 0–10 V, digital.

Humidity

Capacitive polymer sensors, combined T/RH probes. Common in ICH 25 °C/60 % RH and 40 °C/75 % RH chambers.

Light (Photostability)

Lux meters and UVA radiometers for ICH Q1B (1.2 M lux-hours visible + 200 Wh/m² UV).

Other

CO₂, differential pressure, door switches, digital I/O. Chamber types: reach-in, walk-in, photostability, CO₂ incubators.
